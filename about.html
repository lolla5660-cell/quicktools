About QuickTools

QuickTools is a collection of free browser-based tools for everyday tasks.

What we offer

The site brings calculators, text utilities and unit converters together in one simple place. The goal is to make common tasks quick without requiring downloads or an account.

Built for practical use

Each tool is designed around a specific task, with straightforward inputs and results. We aim to keep the interface simple and useful on both desktop and mobile devices.

Accuracy and limitations

QuickTools is intended for general convenience and informational use. For financial, medical, academic or other high-stakes decisions, verify important results with an appropriate authoritative source.
