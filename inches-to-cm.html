About the Inches to CM Converter

Enter a measurement in inches to convert it to centimeters. The standard conversion is 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters.

For example, 10 inches equals 25.4 centimeters.

Frequently Asked Question

How many centimeters are in 1 inch?

There are exactly 2.54 centimeters in 1 inch.
