How to Convert Inches to Centimeters

Inches are commonly used to describe the size of screens, furniture, clothing, and many household products. Centimeters are part of the metric system and are used for everyday measurements in many countries.

To convert inches into centimeters, multiply the measurement in inches by 2.54. This gives you the equivalent length in centimeters.

Inches to CM Conversion Formula

Centimeters = Inches × 2.54

For example, to convert 12 inches:

  • Start with 12 inches.
  • Multiply 12 by 2.54.
  • The result is exactly 30.48 centimeters.

Where Are Inch Measurements Used?

TV and Monitor Screens

Screen sizes are commonly advertised in inches. For example, a 24-inch screen has a diagonal measurement of 60.96 centimeters. Screen size usually refers to the diagonal distance between opposite corners, not the screen width.

Furniture and Home Products

International furniture listings may provide dimensions in inches. Converting these measurements into centimeters makes it easier to compare products and check whether an item fits your available space.

Clothing and Accessories

Some international size guides use inches for waist, length, and other body measurements. Converting inches to centimeters can help when comparing measurements across different size charts.

Inches vs. Centimeters: What Is the Difference?

An inch is a unit of length in the imperial system, while a centimeter is a smaller unit in the metric system. One inch always equals 2.54 centimeters exactly.

Because an inch is longer than a centimeter, the numerical value increases when converting inches into centimeters.
