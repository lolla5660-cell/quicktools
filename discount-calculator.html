How to Calculate a Discount

A discount reduces the original price of a product by a certain percentage. You can use this calculator to find how much money you save and how much you need to pay after the discount.

Discount Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount Percentage ÷ 100)

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example

If an item costs $100 and has a 20% discount:

  • Original price: $100
  • Discount amount: $20
  • Final price: $80

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 20% off a price?

Multiply the original price by 0.20 to find the discount amount, then subtract it from the original price.

Does this calculator show how much I save?

Yes. It displays the discount amount, final price, and percentage discount.

Can I calculate a 100% discount?

Yes. A 100% discount means the entire original price is deducted, so the final price is zero.

Can I use this calculator for any currency?

Yes. Enter the price in your preferred currency. The calculator works with the numerical amount and does not require a specific currency.
