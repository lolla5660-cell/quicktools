How to Calculate a Discount

A discount is a reduction in the original price of a product or service. It is usually expressed as a percentage. A discount calculator helps you find the amount you save and the final price you need to pay.

Enter the original price and discount percentage into the calculator above. The result will show the discount amount and the reduced price instantly.

Discount Calculation Formula

You can calculate a discount using two simple steps:

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount Percentage ÷ 100)

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Discount Calculation Example

Imagine you want to buy a product that costs $150 and the store offers a 20% discount.

  • Original price: $150
  • Discount percentage: 20%
  • Discount amount: $30
  • Final price: $120

You save $30 and pay only $120 after applying the discount.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 20% off a price?

Multiply the original price by 0.20 to find the discount amount. Then subtract that amount from the original price.

How much is 30% off $100?

A 30% discount on $100 saves you $30. The final price is $70.

How do I find the final price after a discount?

Subtract the discount amount from the original price. You can also multiply the original price by (1 − discount percentage ÷ 100).

Can I calculate a 100% discount?

Yes. A 100% discount reduces the final price to zero.

Can I use this calculator with any currency?

Yes. Enter the numerical price in your preferred currency. The calculator works with the amount you enter and does not require a specific currency.

Does the calculator show how much money I save?

Yes. It displays the original price, discount percentage, amount saved, and final price.
