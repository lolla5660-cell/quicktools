About the Discount Calculator

Use the discount calculator to find how much you save and what you pay after a percentage discount. Enter the original price and discount rate above.

For example, a 20% discount on $50 saves $10, so the sale price is $40.

Frequently Asked Question

How is a discount calculated?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage divided by 100. Subtract the discount amount from the original price.
