About the Percentage Calculator

A percentage calculator helps you find a percentage of a number without doing the math by hand. Enter a percentage and a number above to get the result instantly.

To calculate a percentage manually, convert the percentage to a decimal and multiply it by the number. For example, 20% of 50 is 0.20 × 50 = 10.

Frequently Asked Question

How do I calculate a percentage?

Multiply the number by the percentage divided by 100. For example, 15% of 200 is 30.
