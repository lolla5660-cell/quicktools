How to Calculate a Percentage

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. It is commonly used in shopping, school grades, financial calculations, statistics, and everyday situations.

To find a percentage of a number, divide the percentage by 100 and multiply the result by the original number. This calculator performs the calculation automatically.

Percentage Calculation Formula

Percentage of a Number = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

For example, to calculate 20% of 150:

  • Start with 20% and divide it by 100.
  • 20 ÷ 100 = 0.20.
  • Multiply 0.20 by 150.
  • The result is 30.

Percentage Calculation Examples

Here are some common percentage calculations using different numbers.

  • 10% of 200 = 20
  • 15% of 100 = 15
  • 20% of 150 = 30
  • 25% of 80 = 20
  • 50% of 250 = 125
  • 75% of 400 = 300

Where Are Percentages Used?

Shopping and Discounts

Percentages help shoppers understand sale offers, price reductions, and promotional deals. You can use a percentage calculation to find how much a discount represents of the original price.

School and Exam Results

Students and teachers use percentages to calculate exam scores. For example, scoring 45 out of 50 points represents 90% of the total marks.

Business and Finance

Businesses use percentages to calculate profit margins, changes in sales, expenses, and financial ratios.

Everyday Calculations

Percentages are useful for comparing quantities, understanding statistics, and working with proportions in everyday tasks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply it by the number. For example, 20% of 150 is 30.

What is 10% of 100?

10% of 100 is 10 because 10 divided by 100 and multiplied by 100 equals 10.

What is 25% of 200?

25% of 200 is 50.

How do I convert a percentage to a decimal?

Divide the percentage by 100. For example, 25% becomes 0.25, and 50% becomes 0.50.

Can a percentage be greater than 100?

Yes. A percentage can be greater than 100 when the value represents more than the original whole.

Can I calculate a percentage of a decimal number?

Yes. Enter any decimal number into the calculator. The calculation works with whole numbers and decimal values.
