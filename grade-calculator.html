Grade Calculator

Calculate a weighted course grade from a score and its weight.

About the Grade Calculator

Enter a score and the weight assigned to it to calculate its contribution to your overall course grade. This is useful when assignments, exams and projects have different weights.

For example, a score of 90% with a 20% weight contributes 18 percentage points.

Frequently Asked Question

What does weighted contribution mean?

It is the amount a particular score contributes to the final grade after its assigned weight is applied.
