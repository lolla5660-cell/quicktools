GPA Calculator

Enter your course grade points and credit hours to calculate your weighted GPA.

About the GPA Calculator

Add each course with its grade points and credit hours. The calculator uses a weighted average, so courses with more credit hours contribute more to the result.

You can add or remove course rows as needed before calculating the GPA.

Frequently Asked Question

How is weighted GPA calculated?

Multiply each course's grade points by its credit hours, add those values, then divide by the total credit hours.
