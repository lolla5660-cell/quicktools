Compound Interest Calculator

Estimate how your savings or investment could grow over time with compound interest and regular contributions.

What Is Compound Interest?

Compound interest is interest earned on both your original principal and the interest that has accumulated over time. Because previously earned interest can also earn interest, compound growth can become more significant as time passes.

How to Use the Compound Interest Calculator

Enter your initial investment, monthly contribution, estimated annual interest rate and investment period. Then choose how frequently the interest is compounded and select Calculate.

The calculator estimates your future balance based on the information you provide. You can also use the Clear button to start a new calculation.

Compound Interest Formula

For an initial investment with periodic compounding, the standard compound interest formula is:

A = P(1 + r/n)nt

In this formula, A represents the final amount, P is the initial principal, r is the annual interest rate expressed as a decimal, n is the number of compounding periods per year, and t is the number of years.

When regular contributions are made, the calculation also considers those additional deposits over the investment period.

Example of Compound Interest

Suppose you start with $1,000, contribute $100 each month, earn an estimated annual interest rate of 5%, and keep the money invested for 10 years. The calculator can estimate your ending balance, total contributions and estimated interest earned.

Compound Interest vs. Simple Interest

Simple interest is generally calculated only on the original principal. Compound interest, on the other hand, allows previously earned interest to become part of the balance that can earn additional interest.

Over longer periods, this difference can become substantial, especially when interest is compounded regularly and additional contributions are made.

Why Does Compounding Frequency Matter?

Compounding frequency determines how often interest is added to the balance. Depending on the account or investment, interest may be compounded annually, semi-annually, quarterly, monthly or daily.

More frequent compounding can produce a different result because interest is added to the balance at different intervals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is compound interest?

Compound interest is interest calculated on the original amount plus accumulated interest from previous periods.

What is the difference between compound and simple interest?

Simple interest is generally calculated using the original principal, while compound interest also considers accumulated interest.

How often can interest be compounded?

Interest can be compounded at different frequencies, including annually, semi-annually, quarterly, monthly or daily.

Does this calculator guarantee investment returns?

No. The results are estimates based on the values entered. Actual savings or investment returns can vary because of market conditions, fees, taxes, account terms and other factors.

Can I add money every month?

Yes. Enter the amount you expect to contribute each month in the Monthly contribution field. The calculator includes those contributions when estimating the future balance.
