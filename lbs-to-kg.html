How to Convert Pounds to Kilograms

Pounds and kilograms are units used to measure weight or mass. Pounds are commonly used in the United States, while kilograms are the standard unit in most countries that use the metric system.

To convert pounds to kilograms, multiply the weight in pounds by 0.45359237. The result is the equivalent weight in kilograms.

Pounds to KG Conversion Formula

Kilograms = Pounds × 0.45359237

For example, to convert 150 pounds:

  • Start with 150 pounds.
  • Multiply 150 by 0.45359237.
  • The result is approximately 68.04 kilograms.

Why Convert Pounds to Kilograms?

International Weight Measurements

Many international websites and product listings display weight in kilograms. Converting pounds makes it easier to understand measurements when comparing information from different countries.

Travel and Luggage

Some airlines display baggage limits in kilograms, while others use pounds. Knowing how to convert between the two units helps you understand luggage weight restrictions.

Fitness and Everyday Use

Fitness equipment, weight plates, and workout information may use either pounds or kilograms. This converter makes switching between the two measurements simple.
