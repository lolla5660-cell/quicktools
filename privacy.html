Privacy Policy

Overview

QuickTools provides free online calculators, text tools, and converters. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the QuickTools website.

Information Processed by the Tools

Many QuickTools tools are designed to process the information you enter directly in your browser.

For example, text entered into the Word Counter, Case Converter, or Remove Duplicate Lines tools is processed by the page to provide the requested result.

QuickTools does not require you to create an account to use its tools. Unless otherwise stated on a specific page, information entered into the tools is not intentionally submitted to QuickTools for storage.

Cookies and Advertising

QuickTools may use cookies, local storage, web beacons, or similar technologies for website functionality, advertising, and measuring advertising performance.

QuickTools uses Google AdSense to provide advertising when advertising services are enabled on the website. Google and other third-party advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers in connection with advertisements displayed on QuickTools.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Advertising may be personalized based on information such as previous visits, interests, or other permitted signals. Non-personalized ads may also be served based on contextual information.

Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings and may also use other available privacy controls provided by Google or their browser.

Privacy Choices and Consent

Where required by applicable law, QuickTools may display a privacy or consent message that allows visitors to make choices about cookies, local storage, personal data, and personalized advertising.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland may be presented with additional consent choices where required by applicable privacy requirements and Google's policies.

Where required, advertising and consent settings may be managed through Google's privacy and consent tools.

Analytics

QuickTools may use analytics services to understand general website usage, such as which pages are visited and how visitors interact with the website.

If an analytics service is enabled, that service may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Third-Party Services and Links

QuickTools may use third-party services to provide advertising, analytics, hosting, or other website functionality.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. QuickTools is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services.

Children's Privacy

QuickTools is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed at children.

We do not intentionally request personal information from children through the QuickTools tools.

Data Security

QuickTools does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to use its basic calculators, text tools, and converters.

However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information into the tools unless it is necessary for the requested calculation or conversion.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, advertising services, analytics services, or applicable requirements change.

When material changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.

Privacy Questions

QuickTools does not currently provide a separate contact page.

For privacy-related questions or requests, please use the contact or support method made available through the service or platform through which you accessed QuickTools.
