Overview

QuickTools provides free online calculators, text tools, and converters. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the QuickTools website.

Many QuickTools tools are designed to process the information you enter directly in your browser.

QuickTools does not require you to create an account to use its tools. Unless otherwise stated on a specific page, information entered into the tools is not intentionally submitted to QuickTools for storage.
