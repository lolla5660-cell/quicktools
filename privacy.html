Advertising personalization and the use of cookies or similar technologies may depend on your location, applicable laws, your consent choices, and the settings available through the relevant advertising or privacy service. You can also manage certain privacy and advertising preferences through your browser and through controls provided by Google or other relevant service providers. ## Analytics QuickTools may use analytics services to understand general website usage, such as which pages are visited and how visitors interact with the website. QuickTools is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services. ## Children's privacy QuickTools is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed at children. Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information into tools unless it is necessary for the requested calculation or conversion. ## Changes to this Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, advertising services, analytics services, or applicable requirements change.
