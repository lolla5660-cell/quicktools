About the Celsius to Fahrenheit Converter

Enter a temperature in Celsius to convert it to Fahrenheit. The formula is °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32.

For example, 0°C is 32°F and 100°C is 212°F.

Frequently Asked Question

What is the Celsius to Fahrenheit formula?

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 and then add 32.
