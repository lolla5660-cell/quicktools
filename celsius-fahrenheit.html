How to Convert Celsius to Fahrenheit

Celsius and Fahrenheit are two temperature scales used around the world. Celsius is commonly used in most countries, while Fahrenheit is mainly used in the United States.

To convert a temperature from Celsius to Fahrenheit, multiply the Celsius value by 9/5 and then add 32.

Celsius to Fahrenheit Formula

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

For example, to convert 25°C:

  • Multiply 25 by 9/5 to get 45.
  • Add 32 to 45.
  • The result is 77°F.

When Is Celsius to Fahrenheit Conversion Useful?

Temperature conversion is useful when checking weather forecasts, following recipes, reading international instructions, or comparing temperatures from different countries.

For example, a recipe from the United States may list an oven temperature in Fahrenheit, while your oven uses Celsius. Converting the temperature helps you follow the instructions correctly.
